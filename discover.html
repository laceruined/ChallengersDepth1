01 / DID YOU KNOW?

The ocean is stranger than fiction.

From animals that can change color to places where sunlight never reaches, the underwater world is full of surprises. Here are some of the most fascinating facts about Earth's oceans.

FACT 001
🌊

The ocean is enormous.

The ocean covers about 71% of Earth's surface, making it one of the biggest environments on our planet.

FACT 002
🐙

Octopuses have three hearts.

An octopus has three hearts. Two pump blood toward the gills, while the third pumps blood to the rest of the body.

FACT 003
🌑

The deep sea is completely dark.

Sunlight cannot reach the deepest parts of the ocean. Below the sunlit zone, darkness dominates and many animals produce their own light.

FACT 004
✨

Some animals make their own light.

Bioluminescence allows certain marine organisms to produce light through chemical reactions. It can be used for communication, attracting prey, or avoiding predators.

FACT 005
🧊

There are underwater mountains.

The seafloor contains enormous mountain ranges, volcanoes, valleys, trenches, and plains. Much of this landscape is hidden beneath kilometers of water.

FACT 006
🦈

Sharks are ancient.

Sharks have existed for hundreds of millions of years. Their evolutionary history stretches back long before humans appeared.

FACT 007
🌍

We have explored only a fraction of the ocean.

Despite the technology available to modern explorers, huge parts of the ocean remain poorly understood. Every expedition can reveal something new.

And then there's Challenger Deep.

Hidden inside the Mariana Trench is Challenger Deep, the deepest known point in Earth's oceans. It is a place where pressure becomes extraordinary, temperatures are low, and exploration becomes an enormous technological challenge.
